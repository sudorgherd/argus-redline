## 2. Temporary physical fixture

The ADC path used GPIO4, ESP32-S3 ADC1/ADC1_CH3, 12-bit resolution, and Arduino
`ADC_11db` attenuation. The temporary board-powered divider was:

```text
Heltec 3V3
    |
   4.7 kΩ resistor
    |
B10K 10 kΩ potentiometer
    |
   GND

potentiometer wiper
    |
GPIO4 / ADC1_CH3
```

No external supply was used. The Node provided 3V3 and common ground. Before
GPIO4 was connected, the full potentiometer travel was checked with a
multimeter at approximately 0.0–2.3 V, below the project characterization
ceiling of 2.5 V. GPIO4-to-GND voltage was remeasured before every ADC action.
The multimeter is the authoritative voltage reference.

## 3. ADC results

The temporary serial interface emitted:

```text
CAPVAL role=NODE action=analog raw=<raw> norm=<normalized> calibrated_mv=UNAVAILABLE
CAPVAL role=NODE action=analog status=OK type=4 value=<normalized> us=<duration>
```

Normalization used the existing ADC code-space conversion:

```text
floor(raw * 65535 / 4095)
```

| Multimeter voltage | Raw ADC | Normalized uint16 | Dispatch duration |
| -----------------: | ------: | ----------------: | ----------------: |
|             0.51 V |     569 |              9106 |            403 us |
|             1.02 V |    1165 |             18644 |            229 us |
|             1.50 V |    1734 |             27750 |            205 us |
|             2.00 V |    2335 |             37368 |            209 us |
|             2.31 V |    2713 |             43417 |            211 us |

All five operations returned `status=OK`. Raw and normalized values increased
monotonically with the measured voltage; no discontinuity or reversal was
observed over the tested 0.51–2.31 V range. The longest observed dispatch was
403 us.

Physical characterization demonstrated monotonic GPIO4 / ADC1_CH3 response
over the tested 0.51–2.31 V range using the approved temporary
characterization build. Normalized values are not volts. No calibrated-voltage
claim, precision/accuracy claim, guaranteed interpolation or extrapolation,
full ESP32-S3 ADC characterization, or behavior above 2.31 V is established.
The 2.5 V project ceiling remains a characterization constraint, not an
absolute ESP32-S3 or Heltec ADC maximum rating.

## 4. Formal post-characterization radio regression

After the measurements, the Hub was powered and ordinary TEST/ACK traffic
resumed while the Node remained on the characterization build and the ADC
fixture remained connected. Both normal LoRa antennas were attached.

The formal window covered sequences 1 through 30. Every exchange contained a
matching zero-payload `TEST=100` COMMAND from Hub `0x01` to Node `0x10`, a
one-byte ACK from Node to Hub with the same sequence/opcode, and `COMMAND OK`.

```text
30/30 consecutive matching TEST/ACK exchanges — PASS
formal sequence range: 1–30
Node RSSI range: -65 dBm to -60 dBm
Node SNR range: 1.25 dB to 5.50 dB
```

Within the formal window, no retry, ACK timeout, malformed packet, sequence
mismatch, reset, radio error, or receive-restoration failure was observed.
Sequence 31 also completed after the formal window, and sequence 32 had begun
when the excerpt ended; neither is included in the formal 30/30 claim.

This is post-characterization coexistence evidence. It does not alter Wire
Protocol, retry/duplicate behavior, packet types, opcodes, or radio timing.

## 5. Fixture teardown

After qualification, both boards were powered off. The GPIO4/wiper connection,
potentiometer, 4.7 kΩ resistor, and temporary 3V3/GND jumpers were removed.
The boards were returned to normal hardware configuration and powered on.
Temporary ADC fixture teardown is complete. No additional post-teardown radio
regression is claimed by this record.
